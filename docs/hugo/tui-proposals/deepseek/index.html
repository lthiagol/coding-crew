hugo · the TUI for the flow

ten readings of one screen

All ten draw the same state: sixteen queued tasks, TSK29 at the head of the queue, TSK16 in the round, three agents — one per role. One sidebar for the configuration, one main pane for what is running, no tabs.

  • 01-policy-sheet.html · the policy sheet — The order is the widest thing on screen, and the run is read under it as one document.
  • 02-crew-roster.html · the crew roster — The crew is the first block: who is live, what each holds, and for how long.
  • 03-pipeline.html · the pipeline — The queue is a board of statuses: every item sits once, under the status it is in.
  • 04-the-round.html · the round on the loop — The current round is drawn on the lifecycle, stage by stage, with what each stage already proved.
  • 05-review-ledger.html · the review ledger — The review budget is the table: every task, the external rounds it spent, the verdict.
  • 06-seats.html · the seats — The crew is a seat per role, and the composition — one per role, two, one — is the sidebar.
  • 07-findings-desk.html · the findings desk — The finding that blocks the round sits beside the whole queue, one item per line.
  • 08-stamp.html · the stamp — The stamp answers whether the automation runs and why before anything else is read.
  • 09-index-and-flow.html · the index and its flow — The queue is an index; one selected item's round, budget and findings are read beside it.
  • 10-inspector.html · the inspector — The configuration is a form: one field open, its meaning and its gates laid out, the run in view.
